Terms of Service: Player Management

Player Management ("the bot") is the registration and transfer bot of the German Virtual League (GVL). It is a private bot and runs only on the league's own Discord servers. By using the bot, being registered through it, or being a member of a Discord server on which it runs, you agree to the following terms.

What the bot does

The bot registers players and team staff (Team Owners, Trainers and Capos), handles transfers, loans, contract extensions and releases, manages team transfer funds and keeps team and rank roles in sync with the league database. How your data is used is described in our Privacy Policy.

Use of the bot

  • Registration, transfer and staff commands are intended for the League Board, league staff and club representatives. Access depends on your roles on the league's Discord servers.
  • Requests posted in the release channels and in the transfer and contract extension forums must follow the given template and must be correct and complete.
  • You must not register with a Discord or Roblox account that is not your own, register more than one account, or provide false information.
  • You must meet the minimum age required by Discord and Roblox in your country.

League rules and contracts

  • Registrations, transfers and contracts are subject to the rules of the German Virtual League. The League Board may accept, deny or reverse any request.
  • A running contract binds you to your club until it ends. If you ask for your data to be deleted during a contract, your registration is kept until the contract has ended, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting your registration means you can no longer take part in the league as a player.

Abuse

Abuse of the bot, attempts to get around league rules or contracts (for example by using another account), or use of the bot for illegal purposes can lead to restricted access, removal from the league or a ban.
