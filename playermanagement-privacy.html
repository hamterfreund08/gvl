Privacy Policy: Player Management

Player Management ("the bot") is the registration and transfer bot of the German Virtual League (GVL). It is a private bot and runs only on the league's own Discord servers.

Data we collect

  • Player registration: Discord user ID and username, linked Roblox username and Roblox user ID, current team, contract type, contract length, registration status and the staff member who approved the registration.
  • Team staff: Discord user ID and username, Roblox username and ID, team and rank of Team Owners, Managers/Trainers and Capos.
  • Transfers and contracts: transfers, loans, transfer fees, contract extensions, releases, team transfer funds and the staff members who accepted or denied a request.
  • Members and roles: the member list and current role assignments of our league server, used to give and remove team and rank roles and to find users by their Discord username.
  • Message content: read only in the release channels and in the transfer and contract extension forums, in order to create release, transfer and extension requests. Messages in the release channels are deleted by the bot after they are read. The message text itself is not stored; only the outcome of an accepted request (for example a new team, contract length or transfer fee) is saved.

How we use this data

Data is used only to operate the bot's features: player registration, transfers and contracts, team staff management, role synchronisation and the /player and /team lookups.

We do not sell this data or use it for any purpose outside the bot's operation.

Third-party services

  • Google (Sheets / Apps Script): the GVL league database, including registrations, team staff, transfers and transfer logs, is stored there.
  • Roblox: public Roblox usernames and user IDs are looked up to validate registrations and keep usernames up to date.

Data is not shared with any other third parties.

Storage and data retention

Registration, team and transfer records are kept for as long as they are needed for league administration.

Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your data by contacting the league staff on our Discord server or by contacting us below.

Deleting your data means you are removed from the league, as we cannot manage a registration without it. To prevent abuse, for example avoiding a suspension or re-registering with another club, we keep your Discord user ID, Roblox user ID and Roblox username on an internal block list and do not allow a new registration. These values are stored only as one-way hashes, so they cannot be read from the list, and the list contains no other data.
