Privacy Policy – GVL Management

GVL Management ("the bot") is the internal league administration bot of the German Virtual League (GVL). It is used exclusively for managing the league and runs only on the league's own Discord servers.

Data we collect

  • Suspensions: Discord user ID and username, linked Roblox username and ID, reason, offense level, status, and the staff member who issued the suspension.
  • Matches: data extracted from match reports and fixture announcements (teams, score, scorers, assists, cards, referee names, an optional note) and the name of the staff member who created the fixture.
  • Roles and membership: member join events and current role assignments, used to apply the suspension role and to keep referee and suspension roles in sync.
  • Message content: read only in two dedicated staff channels (fixture announcements and match logs) in order to extract the match data above. Raw messages are not stored or logged.

How we use this data

Data is used only to operate the bot's features: fixture and match result management, match notifications, the suspension system, role synchronisation, and the team and stadium overviews.

We do not sell this data or use it for any purpose outside the bot's operation.

Storage and data retention

The data is stored in the league's database, which is hosted on Google services (Google Sheets). It is not shared with other third parties. Suspension and match records are kept for as long as they are needed for league administration.
