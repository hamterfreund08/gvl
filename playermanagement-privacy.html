Privacy Policy: Player Management

Player Management ("the bot") is the registration and transfer bot of the German Virtual League (GVL). It is a private bot and runs only on the league's own Discord servers. This policy explains which data the bot processes, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The controller for the data processed by the bot is the German Virtual League (GVL). You can reach us at frankfurt.roblox@gmail.com or through the league staff on our Discord server.

Data we collect

  • Player registration: Discord user ID and username, linked Roblox username and Roblox user ID, current team, contract type, contract length, registration status, date of registration and the staff member who approved the registration.
  • Team staff: Discord user ID and username, Roblox username and ID, team and rank of Team Owners, Managers/Trainers and Capos.
  • Transfers and contracts: transfers, loans, transfer fees, contract extensions, releases, team transfer funds and the staff members who accepted or denied a request.
  • Members and roles: the member list and current role assignments of our league server, used to give and remove team and rank roles and to find users by their Discord username.
  • Message content: read only in the release channels and in the transfer and contract extension forums, in order to create release, transfer and extension requests. Messages in the release channels are deleted by the bot after they are read. The message text itself is not stored; only the outcome of an accepted request (for example a new team, contract length or transfer fee) is saved.

Purpose and legal basis

  • Running your registration and contract (registration, transfers, loans, contract extensions, releases, team roles): necessary to take part in the league as a player or team staff member, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Fair competition and league administration (transfer rules, contract periods, a complete transfer history, preventing players from leaving a running contract or getting around league rules): our legitimate interest in running a fair league, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Role synchronisation and lookups (/player, /team, keeping usernames up to date): our legitimate interest in keeping the league servers organised, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising and do not use it to train AI models.

Recipients and third-party services

  • Discord: the bot runs on Discord, so all interactions are also processed by Discord under its own privacy policy.
  • Google (Sheets / Apps Script): the league database, including registrations, team staff, transfers and transfer logs, is stored there. Google may process data in the United States. Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers.
  • Roblox: public Roblox usernames and user IDs are looked up to validate registrations and keep usernames up to date.
  • League staff: registration and transfer data is visible to the league staff and, in part, publicly on the league servers (for example transfer announcements and the /player and /team commands).

Data is not shared with any other third parties.

How long we keep your data

  • Registration data is kept for as long as you are registered in the league.
  • Deletion requests during a contract: if you ask for deletion while you are under contract with a club, we keep your registration until your contract has ended and delete it afterwards. This prevents players from leaving a running contract and registering with another club. Until then, your data is only used to manage your existing registration.
  • Transfer and contract history (who moved from which club to which club, when, and on which terms) is kept as part of the league history, because transfer rules and the fairness of the competition depend on a complete record. You can object to this at any time (see below); we will then check whether our interest in keeping the record outweighs yours.
  • Message content is not stored. Messages in the release channels are deleted after the bot has read them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get access to the data we hold about you (Art. 15),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17), within the limits described above,
  • have the processing restricted (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21),
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live.

To use these rights, contact us at the address above or through the league staff. We answer within one month. If we cannot delete your data yet (for example during a running contract), we will tell you why and when it will be deleted.

Deleting your registration means you can no longer take part in the league as a player, because a registration cannot be managed without this data.

Minimum age

To use the bot you must meet the minimum age required by Discord and Roblox in your country.
