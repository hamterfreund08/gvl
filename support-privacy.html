Privacy Policy – GVL Support

Support ("the bot") is the ticket system of the German Virtual League (GVL). Members open a support ticket from a panel on our Discord servers and talk to the support team through direct messages with the bot.

Data we collect

  • Ticket messages: messages and attachments you send to the bot in DMs while you have an open ticket, and messages the support team writes in the ticket channel. They are forwarded between you and the team.
  • Ticket data: ticket ID, category, description, your Discord user ID and username, added users, the staff member who claimed or closed the ticket, timestamps and the close reason.
  • League data: when a ticket is opened, the bot looks up your linked Roblox account, team and active suspensions in the GVL database, so staff can help you faster.
  • Requests: for account swaps and other change requests, the details you enter (e.g. Roblox or Discord account names).
  • Members and roles: used to find users who are added to a ticket and to check staff permissions.

How we use this data

Data is used only to operate the ticket system: forwarding messages, managing tickets, processing requests and keeping a record of support cases.

We do not sell this data or use it for any purpose outside the bot's operation.

Third-party services

  • Google (Sheets / Apps Script): ticket data, requests and the GVL league database are stored there.
  • Google Translate / MyMemory: only when a staff member clicks a translate button, the text of that message is sent there to translate it.
  • Roblox: public Roblox usernames and user IDs are looked up for account requests.

Data is not shared with any other third parties.

Storage and data retention

  • When a ticket is closed, a transcript of the conversation is saved on our own web server for the support team.
  • Ticket records and transcripts are kept for as long as they are needed for league administration and support history.
  • Messages outside of tickets are not read or stored.
